# 🚓 Neon Bay Rush

![Neon Bay Rush](cover.png)

A fast arcade car-chase game set on the glowing streets of a retro neon city.
Weave through traffic, grab cash and outrun the cops. The longer you survive, the higher your wanted level climbs!

## ▶️ Play
**[Play Neon Bay Rush](https://USERNAME.github.io/neon-bay-rush/)**

## 🎮 Controls
| | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Drive | Arrow keys / WASD | Drag your finger |
| Nitro | Space | NITRO button |
| Play again | Enter | Tap button |

## ✨ Features
- Endless chase with a 5-star wanted system
- Nitro boost: smash through traffic and police cars
- Trick cops into crashing for bonus cash
- Neon synthwave city that fills any screen, landscape or portrait
- High score saved in your browser

## 🛠️ Built with
Plain HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript. No engine, single file, no external images or sounds.
